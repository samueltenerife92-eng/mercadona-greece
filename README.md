# Mercadona Greece: Market Entry Analysis

## Project Overview

This project evaluates the potential market entry of Mercadona into Greece using PostgreSQL and a business-oriented analytical framework.

The objective is to compare selected Greek cities, examine the competitive landscape, and explore illustrative financial scenarios to support an initial market-entry assessment.

## Business Questions

- Which Greek cities should be prioritized for further market research?
- What does the selected competitor sample reveal about the local supermarket landscape?
- How sensitive are illustrative financial outcomes to market capture and operating costs?
- What additional data would be required before making an investment decision?

## Geographic Scope

- Athens
- Thessaloniki
- Patras
- Heraklion

## Technical Skills

- PostgreSQL and relational database design
- SQL joins, aggregations, and subqueries
- Market sizing and city-level comparisons
- Competitor mapping and distance analysis
- Financial scenario and break-even analysis
- Data quality checks and assumption documentation

## Preliminary Findings

Athens emerges as the first city to investigate further, followed by Thessaloniki, Patras, and Heraklion under the current illustrative assumptions.

These results are preliminary hypotheses, not investment recommendations. Market estimates, competitor locations, and financial assumptions require further validation.

## Limitations

- Grocery spending uses a national-average proxy rather than verified city-level spending data.
- Store locations and competitor coverage require additional verification.
- Financial scenarios are illustrative and do not represent Mercadona's actual costs, sales, or forecasts.
- The model excludes some investment and financing considerations.
