
# Market Entry Findings and Limitations

## Objective

Evaluate which Greek cities merit further investigation for a potential Mercadona market entry, using a preliminary market-sizing model, a competitor sample, and illustrative financial scenarios.

## Preliminary Market Prioritization

The current model suggests the following order for further research:

1. Athens
2. Thessaloniki
3. Patras
4. Heraklion

This ranking is a preliminary hypothesis based on estimated market size and assumed operating costs. It is not an investment recommendation.

## Financial Model

The model estimates monthly grocery market size using target households multiplied by estimated monthly grocery spending per household.

Break-even sales are calculated by dividing monthly operating costs by the assumed gross margin rate.

The scenarios are illustrative and do not represent Mercadona's actual costs, margins, sales, or business plans.

## Limitations

- Household counts and market estimates require source-level verification.
- The €357 monthly grocery-spending figure is a national-average proxy, not a city-specific measurement.
- Competitor store addresses and coordinates are candidate data and may be approximate.
- Straight-line distances are not driving or walking distances.
- A city-wide market capture rate is not a reliable store-level sales forecast.
- The financial model excludes capital expenditure, tax, depreciation, financing, and other potential costs.
- The model should be refined with validated local data before supporting an actual investment decision.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. Verify household and demographic data using official sources.
2. Validate each competitor store's exact address and coordinates.
3. Obtain more reliable city-level grocery expenditure data.
4. Test multiple margin, rent, staffing, and sales scenarios.
5. Incorporate store catchment areas and realistic store-level demand estimates.
